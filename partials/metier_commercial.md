# Domain reference: Sales

Job family covered: Field Sales Representative, Business Developer, Account Manager, Key Account Manager, Sales Manager, Sales Director.

---

## Core competencies (x3)

| Competency | Junior / Field Sales | Experienced / Account Manager | Senior / KAM | Sales Leadership |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mastery of the sales cycle | Prospects, qualifies, presents the offer. Manages a simple pipeline. | Leads complex, multi-stakeholder cycles. Masters negotiation. | Manages strategic accounts, builds account plans, leads tenders (appels d'offres). | Defines the sales strategy, structures sales processes, owns the forecast. |
| Negotiation & closing | Negotiates standard terms. Knows when to escalate. | Negotiates complex agreements (multi-lot, multi-year). Handles substantive objections. | Negotiates at C-level. Builds structured deals (partnerships, co-development). | Defines the pricing policy, arbitrates strategic deals, manages escalations. |
| Market & customer knowledge | Understands the offer and positioning. Identifies customer needs. | Masters the competitive ecosystem. Anticipates market changes within their scope. | Deep sector expertise. Identifies growth opportunities in key accounts. | 3-5 year market vision. Steers product strategy through field feedback. |

## Secondary competencies (x2)

| Competency | Junior | Experienced | Senior | Leadership |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Data-driven management | Tracks their own indicators (revenue, pipeline, conversion rate). | Analyzes their activity, identifies performance levers. | Manages a complex budget/target, forecasts accurately. | Defines the KPIs, manages the sales P&L, reports to the CODIR (comité de direction, executive committee). |
| Internal collaboration | Works with pre-sales and delivery. | Coordinates project teams on large deals. | Aligns sales, marketing, product and leadership on strategic accounts. | Structures sales/marketing/product collaboration at scale. |

## Complementary competencies (x1)

| Competency | Description |
|---|---|
| CRM & tools proficiency | Uses the CRM (Salesforce, HubSpot...) rigorously. Data up to date, pipeline qualified. |
| Presentation & storytelling | Builds impactful presentations adapted to the audience. |
| Stress management & resilience | Handles target pressure, bounces back after a lost sale. |

---

## Recommended role-play scenarios

- **Negotiation simulation**: present an offer to a "customer" who raises objections on price, schedule and competition. Observe: active listening, rephrasing, ability to defend value without discounting.
- **Pipeline analysis**: present a fictional sales pipeline and ask the candidate to identify at-risk deals, priority actions and a realistic forecast. Observe: analytical rigor, realism about probabilities, ability to prioritize.

## Red flags

### Red
- Unable to explain a lost deal without blaming the customer or the product
- No structure in the sales approach (no methodology, no process)
- Vague or unverifiable performance figures

### Orange
- Exclusive focus on closing with no long-term relationship vision
- Difficulty adapting their pitch to different levels of stakeholder
- No curiosity about the product/service being sold
